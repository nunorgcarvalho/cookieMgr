- **Auto buyer, To next achievement** (profile setting, on by default): besides its bulk, each building is also
  measured bought up to the next achievement it wins (within 100), and the queue takes that count when its
  payback period is better. A building a few short of a tier now gets credit for the milk that tier brings,
  spread over the whole buy. Its queue row carries a "to next achievement" badge.
